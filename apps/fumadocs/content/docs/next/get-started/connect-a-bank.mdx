---
title: Connect a bank
description: Create a free Powens sandbox, enter it on the setup screen, and link a test bank so your budget fills.
icon: Landmark
---

Transactions only reach Freenary through a bank connection, so this step makes it useful. You'll use a free Powens sandbox and its test bank, so you don't need real bank credentials yet. You need to be the operator for this page.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create a Powens sandbox

Everything here happens in the [Powens console](https://console.powens.com). A sandbox domain is free and needs no contract.

1. [Sign up](https://console.powens.com/auth/register), confirm your email, and fill in the organization details.
2. Create a domain. Powens adds `-sandbox` to the name, so `acme` becomes `acme-sandbox`. Note the full name.
3. Open the domain's **Client Applications**, press **Add a client**, and register this redirect URL, with your own hostname:

   ```text
   https://freenary.example.com/callback/powens
   ```

   Powens sends you back to this address after the bank sign-in. It's your instance's address followed by `/callback/powens`, character for character.

4. Note the client ID and the client secret.
5. Activate the connectors (banks) you want to offer, or all of them. With no active connector, the bank list in Freenary stays empty even when the credentials are right.

Powens' console changes over time, so a label may differ from this list. Its [quick start](https://docs.powens.com/documentation/integration-guides/quick-start) has the current steps.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter it on the setup screen

Go back to the setup screen, or open `https://freenary.example.com/setup`.

1. On the **Bank** step, pick **Powens** and press **Continue**. The form repeats the steps above and shows your redirect URL with a **Copy** button.
2. Fill in **Powens domain** (for example `acme-sandbox`), **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
3. Press **Save and continue**. Freenary tests the credentials with Powens first. If Powens refuses them, the step says so and you can correct them.
4. On the **Categories** step, pick a model and fill in its settings, or choose **Skip for now** and press **Continue**. Without a model, a transaction that no rule, dictionary entry or correction recognises stays uncategorised. See [AI models](/docs/run/models).
5. On **Done**, check that **Bank provider** shows Powens. Email and the assistant aren't part of the setup screen: see [Email](/docs/run/email) and [AI models](/docs/run/models#assistant).
6. Press **Finish setup**. The server restarts to apply the credentials: the page shows **Applying your settings…** and reconnects on its own. If it shows **The server did not restart** instead, see [The server does not restart after setup](/docs/run/troubleshooting#the-server-does-not-restart-after-setup).

</Step>

<Step>

### Link the test bank

Freenary now asks two questions.

1. **Tax residency.** Pick every country you pay tax in, then press **Continue**. These countries decide which banks the list shows, and you can't [change them later](/docs/use/account#first-steps). Countries other than France show **Limited support**.
2. **Connect your bank.** Search for `Connecteur de test`, the test bank Powens offers in every domain, and press **Connect**.
3. On the Powens page, sign in with any user name and the password `1234`.
4. Freenary brings you back to the same step with a message such as **Connected 2 accounts**. Press **Finish (1)** to open Freenary.

</Step>

<Step>

### See your budget fill

Freenary syncs the new connection as soon as you finish, and a message in the corner of the screen shows how far it got. Open **Budget**: the test transactions appear, each one sorted into a category.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">

**The test bank isn't in the list?** Check that it's active in your Powens domain. For an empty list, see [The bank list is empty](/docs/run/troubleshooting#the-bank-list-is-empty).

</Callout>

When you're ready for your real banks, connect them from **Settings**, see [Bank connections](/docs/use/bank-connections). A sandbox domain holds a limited number of connections, and a production domain needs a contract with Powens. For Enable Banking, the other bank provider, see [Bank providers](/docs/reference/bank-providers).
