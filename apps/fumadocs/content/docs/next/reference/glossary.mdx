Each word means the same thing on every page and in the app.

## Instance and people

- **Instance**: your installed copy of Freenary, with its own accounts and data.
- **Operator**: the first account on the instance, and the only one that can open the setup screen.
- **Setup**: the screen at `/setup` where the operator picks a **Bank provider** and an email service.
- **Tax residency**: the countries you pay tax in, picked once when you first sign in. They decide which banks you can connect.
- **Limited support**: a tax residency country Freenary supports only in part. See [Your account](/docs/use/account#first-steps).
- **Planned**: a sidebar item for a screen that doesn't exist yet.
- **First steps**: the checklist at the bottom of the sidebar until you've connected a bank, set a plan and protected your account.
- **One-time code**: a code Freenary emails you to confirm your address or reset your password.
- **Passkey**: a key stored on your device, so you sign in with your device unlock instead of a password.
- **Two-factor authentication**: a code from your authenticator app on top of your password. A **Recovery code** replaces a lost app.

## Banks

- **Bank provider**: the service that links Freenary to banks, Powens or Enable Banking.
- **Bank accounts**: the card under Settings, `Connections`, that lists your connected banks and their accounts.
- **Connect**: start linking a bank. You sign in at your bank, then come back to Freenary.
- **Sync transactions**: fetch new transactions from your banks. Opening Budget does it too.
- **Disconnect**: remove a bank, its accounts and their transactions from Freenary.

## Budget

- **Transactions**: what came in (**Incoming**) and went out (**Outgoing**) over a period, by category.
- **Company**: the other party of a transaction, read from the bank's description.
- **Category**: the label a transaction carries, such as `Groceries`. **Other** is the one it gets when nothing fits.
- **Income, Savings & investment, Spending**: the three fixed groups every category sits in.
- **Custom category**: a category you create, with your own name and icon.
- **Budgeting profile**: your monthly plan, one amount per category. Each amount is a **budget line**.
- **Budget vs. actual**: your plan beside what you spent.
- **1M, 3M, 1Y**: the period Budget shows. On `3M` and `1Y`, **Total**, **Monthly avg.** and **Monthly median** change how figures are counted.
- **Fixed, Variable**: spending at companies Freenary sees as recurring, and the rest.
- **Recurring**: the page that lists payments that repeat over your last 12 months.
- **Commitments**: payments to one company on a regular schedule for a steady amount, such as rent.
- **Spending patterns**: purchases you repeat at one company without a regular schedule or amount.
- **Confidence**: how sure Freenary is of a recurring payment: **Confirmed**, **Likely** or **Pattern**.

## Assistant

- **Assistant**: the question box on Home that answers from your own data.
- **On your instance**: a model the operator set up for everyone.
- **On this device**: a model that runs in your browser on your graphics card.
- **New conversation**: start over. The previous conversation isn't shown again.
- **How the assistant answered**: the steps it took to find an answer.
