Freenary is an open-source personal finance app you host yourself. It connects to your banks, imports your accounts and transactions, sorts each transaction into a category, and shows how your spending compares with your plan. It runs on your own server, and your data stays in your own PostgreSQL database.

## Who it's for

Freenary is for people who want to see where their money goes without handing their bank data to a third-party app. You install it once on a server, for yourself or your household. The person who installs it is the **operator**. Everyone else just signs in and uses it.

## What you get

- **Bank sync.** Link your banks through a bank provider (Powens or Enable Banking). Accounts and transactions arrive on their own.
- **Automatic categories.** Each transaction lands in a category, and you can correct it or add your own.
- **A budget.** Plan what you want to spend each period, then compare it with what you actually spent.
- **An assistant.** Ask questions about your money in plain language, like "Where did my money go last month?"
- **Your data, your server.** Everything lives in your instance's database. One exception: when you ask the assistant a question, Freenary sends the data it needs to the model endpoint the operator configured. Read [The assistant](/docs/use/assistant) to see what that means for your instance.

## Status

Freenary is young. The **Budget** screen works today. **Portfolio**, **Analysis** and **Goals** are planned.
