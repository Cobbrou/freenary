This page covers your own account: getting started, signing in, keeping it safe, and changing Freenary's language and look.

## First steps

The first time you sign in, Freenary asks two short questions:

1. **Your tax residency.** Pick every country you pay tax in. It decides which banks you can connect and how transactions get sorted. France has full support; other countries are marked `Limited support`, so fewer transactions get a category automatically.
2. **Your bank.** Connect a bank now, or press `Skip for now` and do it later from Settings.

If you're the operator, the [setup screen](/docs/get-started/connect-a-bank) opens before these questions.

Then a **First steps** checklist appears at the bottom of the sidebar. Each item links to the right place:

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| `Connect your bank` | You have at least one [bank connection](/docs/use/bank-connections) |
| `Set up your budget` | Your [plan](/docs/use/budget#plan-a-monthly-amount) has at least one line |
| `Protect your account` | You turn on two-factor authentication or add a passkey |

The checklist disappears once all three are done.

## Sign in

Type your email address on the sign-in screen. Freenary checks it, then asks for your password, or for a name and password if you're new. A new password needs at least 12 characters.

Depending on how your instance is set up, you may also see:

- **A confirmation code.** On an instance that sends email, a new account gets a 6-digit code by email. It's valid for 10 minutes. No code? Press `Send a new code` and check your spam folder.
- **Forgot password?** It emails you a code to choose a new password, then you sign in with it. This link only appears on an instance that sends email.
- **Sign in with a passkey** — use your device's fingerprint, face or PIN instead of a password.
- **Continue with Google**, **Apple** or **single sign-on**, if your operator turned them on.

You stay signed in for 7 days on each browser.

<Callout type="info">

Your operator decides which methods exist and whether Freenary sends email. If a code never arrives or there's no `Forgot password?` link, ask them. Operator details: [Sign-in](/docs/run/sign-in) and [Email](/docs/run/email).

</Callout>

## Protect your account

Do at least one of these, from Settings, `Security`.

### Turn on two-factor authentication

With two-factor on, signing in with your password also asks for a code from an authenticator app.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Start

In `Two-factor authentication`, turn the switch on and confirm your password.

</Step>

<Step>

### Scan the code

Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, then type the 6-digit code it shows.

</Step>

<Step>

### Save your recovery codes

Copy the recovery codes and keep them somewhere safe. Each one works once if you lose your phone.

</Step>

</Steps>

At sign-in you can tick "Remember this device" to skip the code on that browser for 30 days.

Two-factor protects password sign-in only. If you created your account with Google, Apple or single sign-on, you have no password, so add a passkey instead.

### Add a passkey

In `Passkeys`, press `Add a passkey`, name it, and follow your browser's prompt. Passkeys need a recent browser and an HTTPS address.

## Settings

Open Settings from the sidebar. It has four panes:

| Pane | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| `Appearance` | Theme and language |
| `Connections` | Connect, re-sync and disconnect banks |
| `Budget` | Your monthly plan and your categories |
| `Security` | Two-factor, passkeys, signed-in browsers, and linked Google, Apple or single sign-on accounts |

In `Active sessions` you can sign out a single browser with `Revoke`, or all others at once with `Sign out other sessions`.

Freenary has no screen to change your name or email address, or to delete your account. To change your password, use `Forgot password?` on the sign-in screen.

## Language and theme

Change them from Settings, `Appearance`, or from the menu under your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Both are also on the sign-in screen.

- **Language:** English or Français. The page reloads, so save any unsaved plan first.
- **Theme:** `System` (follows your device), `Dark` or `Light`.

Both choices belong to the browser, not to your account.
