The assistant is the question box on Home. Ask it about your accounts, spending or budget, and it answers from the data your banks sent to Freenary. Connect a [bank](/docs/use/bank-connections) first, or it has little to read.

## Ask a question

Type your question on Home and send it, for example:

- How much did I spend on eating out this year?
- Where did my money go last month?
- Which subscriptions am I paying for?

An empty conversation offers these as suggestions. Pick one to fill the box, edit it, then send.

Above each answer, Freenary lists the lookups it made, such as `Searching your transactions`. Open `How the assistant answered` to see where a figure came from. Some answers include a chart. Use `Copy` to copy an answer and `Retry` to ask again.

The assistant only reads. It doesn't change a category or a budget line, doesn't start a sync, and never moves money.

## Pick a model

The model button in the question box shows which model answers. It lists two kinds:

- **`On your instance`**: a model your operator set up. Nothing to download.
- **`On this device`**: a model that runs in your browser, on your graphics card. It downloads once from Hugging Face, and needs WebGPU, which a current Chrome, Edge or Safari has. Answers are simpler than a large hosted model's.

If the box says `Choose a model` and stays greyed out, your instance has no model. Pick one for your device, or ask your operator to turn the assistant on.

## What data goes where

To answer, the model receives your question, the earlier turns of the conversation, today's date, your interface language, the dates your transaction data covers, and what it looked up:

- your account names and their banks;
- up to 20 transactions per lookup: date, amount, currency, the bank's label, the other party and the category;
- spending and cash-flow totals, your plan against what you spent, and the recurring payments Freenary found.

It never receives your IBAN, your account balances, your name or your email address.

Where that goes depends on the model:

- **A model on your instance** sends it to the model service your operator chose. That can be a company outside your instance, under its own terms.
- **A model on this device** keeps it in your browser. Nothing goes to an outside model provider.

<Callout type="info">

Your conversation is saved on your instance with either kind of model, so it's there after a reload and in your other browsers.

</Callout>

## Too many questions

You can ask about 30 questions every 5 minutes, whatever the model. Past that, the assistant says `Too many questions in a row. Wait a moment and try again.`

With a model on this device, the answer still appears, but Freenary doesn't save it to your conversation.

## Start a new conversation

Press `New conversation` to start over. You have one conversation at a time: the previous one stays on your instance, but no screen shows it again.

A long conversation can outgrow a model on this device. When that happens, the assistant asks you to start a new conversation.

Operators turn on a model for the whole instance in [Configuration](/docs/reference/configuration#assistant).
