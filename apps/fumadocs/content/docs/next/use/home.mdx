Home is the screen you land on after you sign in. It opens on a greeting and a question field, then lists your dashboards, your accounts and an overview of this month. It fills once you have a [bank connection](/docs/use/bank-connections); with none, it offers `Connect a bank`.

On a phone, a bar at the bottom of the screen opens **Home**, **Ask**, **Transactions**, **Recurring** and **Settings**. On a wider screen, the sidebar does.

## Ask a question

Under the greeting, type a question in the field that reads `Ask me anything. A new chat opens in Ask.` and press Enter. A new chat opens in **Ask** with your question, and the assistant answers there. See [Ask](/docs/use/ask).

## Dashboards

**Dashboards** shows your three most recently updated dashboards. Each card sketches the dashboard's layout, with its name and when it last changed. Press a card to open it.

The last card asks for a dashboard: it opens **Ask** with a request already started for you to finish. See [Dashboards](/docs/use/dashboards).

## Accounts

**Accounts** shows one tile per account with its name, its bank and its balance in the account's own currency. A dash means the bank hasn't reported a balance. Beside the heading, `{amount} in total` is your [net worth](#net-worth) today. On a narrow screen, scroll the row sideways to see every tile.

Press a tile to open the account: its full name, its bank and kind, its balance and its transactions, newest first. `Show older transactions` loads more. The last tile, `Add an account`, opens the place where you link a bank.

## This month

**Overview** covers this month so far, across all your accounts.

### Spent this month

**Spent this month** adds up every payment out of your accounts since the first of the month. It's the same figure as the expenses on **Transactions** for this month, internal transfers included. The line under it compares your spending so far with the same day last month.

### Available to spend

**Available to spend** is what your current accounts hold, minus the bills still due before the end of the month. Bills are the payments on a regular cadence for a steady amount that **Recurring** lists under `Bills`. Spending patterns, such as groceries, aren't taken off: they are what this amount is for.

The figure turns red, with a minus sign, when your current accounts don't cover the bills due this month.

### Saved this month

**Saved this month** is what came into your accounts this month minus what went out, with the share of your income it represents. When more went out than came in, it turns red and says by how much.

### Spending by week

**Spending by week** stacks your spending over the last seven weeks, week by week. The four categories you spent the most in over those weeks each get a colour; the rest add up under `Other`. Money moved to savings and investments doesn't count as spending here.

### Income vs spending

**Income vs spending** draws money in and money out, month by month, with what you saved, or overspent, over the period. Pick `3M`, `6M` or `12M` to change how many months it covers.

### Budgets

**Budgets** compares this month's spending with each line of your [monthly plan](/docs/use/budget), and counts the days left in the month. A line turns red when you go over. With no plan, `Set up your budget` opens the place where you set one.

### Top categories this month

**Top categories this month** shows the five categories you spent the most in this month, with each one's share of your spending. Press a category to ask why you spent that much on it: a new chat opens in **Ask** with the question.

Spending Freenary hasn't sorted into a category is left out of the list. A line under it gives that amount, and `Review unsorted transactions` opens **Transactions** filtered to it.

### Upcoming bills

**Upcoming bills** lists the bills due in the next 14 days, with how many there are and their total. It shows the first five, soonest first; the rest are on **Recurring**.

### Net worth

**Net worth** is what you own minus what you owe: every account balance added up, with a balance below zero, such as a loan or an overdraft, counted as a debt. The chart draws it at the end of each month, over `6M` or `12M`, and the line under the total gives how much it moved over that time.

Freenary rebuilds the past months from your transactions, starting from today's balances. An account without transactions, such as most investments, counts at its latest value for every month.

## Currencies

Freenary doesn't convert between currencies.

- **Accounts** shows each balance in its own currency. Its total, like **Net worth**, counts only the accounts in the currency most of your accounts use.
- **Available to spend** uses the currency most of your current accounts use, and takes off only the bills in that currency. So does the line above the list on **Upcoming bills**; it's hidden when no bill is in that currency. The list itself shows every bill in its own currency.
- **Spent this month**, **Saved this month**, **Spending by week**, **Income vs spending**, **Budgets** and **Top categories this month** work like **Transactions**: an amount in another currency is added as it is.
