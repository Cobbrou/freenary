Budget shows what came in and what went out over a period, sorted into categories. You can compare it with a monthly plan you set yourself.

Budget fills once you have a bank connection. See [Bank connections](/docs/use/bank-connections).

## Read your budget

Budget has two pages in the sidebar:

- **Transactions** — income, expenses and the difference for the period you pick, two charts, and the list of transactions.
- **Recurring** — subscriptions, rent and other payments that repeat, what they cost per month and per year, and what's due next. It always looks at your last 12 months.

On **Transactions**, pick a range (`1M`, `3M` or `1Y`) and use the arrows to move between periods. On `3M` and `1Y` you can show monthly averages or medians instead of totals.

Press a part of a chart to filter the list to that category. Use the search box and the `Amount`, `Company` and `Category` filters to narrow it further. `Clear all` removes every filter.

<Callout type="info">

The page address holds the period and every filter. Copy it to bookmark or share exactly what you see.

</Callout>

## Get the latest transactions

Freenary syncs your banks when you open Budget. To fetch again, press `Sync transactions`. Transactions appear while the sync runs.

A sync reads at most the last 90 days. Older transactions never arrive. See [Bank connections](/docs/use/bank-connections#sync).

## Change a transaction's category

Freenary sorts each transaction into a category for you. When it gets one wrong:

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the transaction

Press the row in the list. A `Transaction details` panel opens.

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick the right category

Open the category picker and choose. Freenary saves at once.

</Step>

</Steps>

Your choice applies to the company, not only to that one transaction. Other transactions from the same company move to your category, and so do future ones. Transactions you already fixed by hand keep their category.

To undo, press the reset button next to the picker. It puts back the category Freenary chose.

<Callout type="warn">

You can only change the category. Freenary has no way to add, edit or delete a transaction: everything comes from your bank. The picker only offers the built-in categories, not your custom ones.

</Callout>

## Know the categories

Every category belongs to one of three fixed groups:

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Income | Money that comes in: salary, refunds, benefits… |
| Savings & investment | Money you put aside: savings, investments, retirement, crypto |
| Spending | Money you spend: groceries, rent, bills, transport… |

A category takes the colour of its group. To see the full list, open Settings, then `Budget`, then `Categories`.

Money moved between two of your own accounts is recognised as an internal transfer, so it doesn't count twice.

## Add a custom category

Custom categories let you organise your plan the way you think about it, for example "Holidays" or "Kids".

1. Open Settings, then `Budget`.
2. In `Categories`, press `New category`.
3. Give it a name and an icon, and choose where it sits: under a group, under one of your own categories, or on its own.
4. Press `Create category`.

You can nest one level deep: a custom category under another custom category, but no further. A custom category under a group takes that group's colour; one on its own gets a colour you pick.

You can edit, reorder or delete your own categories. Deleting one moves its budget lines to a catch-all category. The built-in categories can't be changed.

Custom categories are for budget lines only. You can't file a transaction under one.

## Plan a monthly amount

Your plan is a list of budget lines: one monthly amount per category. Freenary compares it with what you actually spent.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the plan

Open Settings, then `Budget`. The plan sits under `Budgeting profile`.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add your lines

Press `Add a line`. Pick a category, type the monthly amount in euros, and add a name if you like. Repeat for each thing you want to plan.

The category decides whether a line counts as income, savings or spending.

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

A bar appears at the bottom of the page. Press `Save`.

</Step>

</Steps>

A diagram above the editor shows how your planned income flows into each line, and how much is left or over-allocated.

Your plan holds up to 200 lines. Save before you switch language or leave the page, or you lose your changes.

## Compare plan and actual

On **Transactions**, open the chart menu on the right and choose `Budget vs. actual`. Each category shows what you spent against what you planned, and whether you're over or have some left.

With no plan yet, the chart offers `Set a spending plan`, which opens the plan editor.
