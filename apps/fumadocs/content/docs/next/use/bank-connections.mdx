A bank connection links Freenary to one bank. Your accounts and transactions arrive through it; you never type them in by hand.

You manage connections in Settings, under `Connections`. The same list appears during the first-sign-in steps.

<Callout type="warn">

If the list says `Bank linking is unavailable`, your instance has no bank provider yet. The operator sets one up in the setup screen: see [Connect a bank](/docs/get-started/connect-a-bank).

</Callout>

## Connect a bank

<Steps>

<Step>

### Find your bank

Open Settings, then `Connections`. Search for your bank and press `Connect` on its row.

The list shows banks for the countries you chose as your tax residency.

</Step>

<Step>

### Approve access at your bank

Your browser goes to your bank, or to the service that links it. Sign in there, confirm with your bank's usual security check, and choose which accounts to share.

</Step>

<Step>

### Come back to Freenary

Your bank sends you back. Freenary shows `Bank connected` and starts importing.

</Step>

</Steps>

Freenary imports your accounts, their balances, and your transactions, each sorted into a category. Some bank providers also return investment holdings.

If you cancel at the bank, nothing is linked. Press `Connect` again to retry.

## Sync

A sync fetches new transactions from your banks. There's no nightly sync: Freenary syncs when you ask.

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You open Budget | A quiet sync, once per visit |
| You press `Sync transactions` on Budget | Every connection is read again |
| You press the re-sync button on a bank's row in Settings | That one connection is read again |

Each sync reads at most the last **90 days**. The first sync brings in those 90 days; older transactions never arrive, and nothing widens the window.

While a sync runs, Budget shows its progress and fills in as transactions arrive. Only one sync runs at a time.

## Disconnect a bank

1. In Settings, `Connections`, press `Disconnect` on the bank's row.
2. Confirm with `Disconnect bank`.

Freenary asks your bank to revoke its access, then deletes that connection's accounts and transactions. Your plan, custom categories and category fixes stay.

If Freenary couldn't reach your bank to revoke access, it tells you so. Revoke it from your bank's own app or website to be sure.

## Fix an expired connection

Bank consents don't last forever. When yours runs out, or your bank wants you to confirm again, syncing that bank fails with `Failed to sync transactions` and its "Synced" date stops moving.

There's no reconnect button. Instead:

<Steps>

<Step>

### Try a re-sync

Press the re-sync button on the bank's row. A temporary failure often clears here.

</Step>

<Step>

### Disconnect the bank

If it still fails, disconnect it. Its accounts and transactions are removed with it.

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect it again

Press `Connect` on the same bank and approve access again. This is a new connection, so the last 90 days are imported again.

</Step>

</Steps>

## When something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `Bank linking is unavailable` | Ask your operator to set up a bank provider |
| `Could not check bank linking` or `Could not load banks` | Reload the page |
| `No banks match your search` | Search for a shorter part of the name |
| `Bank connection failed. You can try again.` | Press `Connect` again |
| `Failed to sync transactions` | Press `Retry`. If it keeps failing, [fix the connection](#fix-an-expired-connection) |

Operators: see [Troubleshooting](/docs/run/troubleshooting#the-bank-list-is-empty) for an empty bank list.
