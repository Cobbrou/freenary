A bank connection links Freenary to one bank. Your accounts and transactions arrive through it; you never type them in.

You manage connections in Settings, under `Connections`, where `Bank accounts` lists the banks you connected. The first-sign-in steps show the list of banks to connect.

<Callout type="warn">

If the list says `Bank linking is unavailable`, your instance has no bank provider yet. Ask your operator to set one up: see [Connect a bank](/docs/get-started/connect-a-bank).

</Callout>

## Add a bank

<Steps>

<Step>

### Find your bank

Open Settings, then `Connections`, and press `Connect a bank`. Search for your bank and press `Connect` on its row. The list holds the banks of the countries you chose as your tax residency.

</Step>

<Step>

### Approve access at your bank

Your browser goes to your bank, or to the service that links it. Sign in there, pass your bank's usual security check, and choose the accounts to share.

</Step>

<Step>

### Come back to Freenary

Your bank sends you back to Settings. Freenary says how many accounts it connected, or `Bank connected` if your bank shared none.

</Step>

</Steps>

If you cancel at your bank, Freenary says `Bank connection cancelled. Nothing was linked.` Press `Connect` again whenever you're ready.

## What arrives, and when

Right after you connect, the bank and its accounts are in the list, marked `Not synced yet`. No transaction has arrived yet.

Transactions arrive with the first sync, which starts on its own once you're back in Freenary: in Settings, or when you finish onboarding. The re-sync button on the bank's row starts one too. The first sync reads the last **90 days**. Anything older on the day you connect never arrives.

While a sync runs, a message in the corner of the screen says what it's doing and how far it got, on every page once you're signed in. It goes away when the sync ends. Budget fills in as transactions arrive. Freenary then sorts them into categories; [Budget](/docs/use/budget#fix-a-category) shows how to fix the ones it gets wrong.

## Sync

A sync fetches transactions from your banks. Freenary has no nightly sync: it syncs only when one of these happens.

| You | Freenary |
| --- | --- |
| Sign in, finish onboarding, or load or reload a page | Runs a quiet sync of every bank. It fetches what's new since the last sync and sorts only the new transactions. Moving between pages doesn't start another one |
| Press `Sync transactions` on Budget | Reads the last 90 days again from every bank, and sorts all your transactions into categories again |
| Press the re-sync button on a bank's row in Settings | Does the same for that one bank |

Sorting again keeps every category you fixed by hand.

When some of a bank's accounts fail, its last sync date still moves forward. Press `Sync transactions` to fill the gap.

One sync runs at a time. A sync you start while another is running does nothing, even when the bank's row then says it re-synced. Wait until the sync message goes away, then start it again.

## Remove a bank

1. In Settings, `Connections`, press `Disconnect` on the bank's row.
2. Confirm with `Disconnect bank`.

Freenary asks your bank to revoke its access, then deletes the connection with its accounts and transactions. Your plan, your custom categories and your company-wide category fixes stay.

<Callout type="warn">

If Freenary couldn't reach your bank, it says the bank was disconnected but access could not be revoked. Revoke it from your bank's own app or website.

</Callout>

To connect the same bank again later, press `Connect` on it. It's a new connection, so it reads the last 90 days again.

## Error messages

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `Bank linking is unavailable` | Ask your operator to set up a bank provider |
| `Could not check bank linking`, `Could not load banks` or `Could not load your banks` | Reload the page |
| `Freenary could not load banks for` a country | Reload the page. If it stays, ask your operator |
| `No banks match your search` | Search for a shorter part of the name |
| `Could not connect to` your bank, `Try again later.` | Wait a few minutes, then press `Connect` again |
| `Bank connection failed. You can try again.` or `The bank sent back an incomplete response.` | Press `Connect` again |
| `Failed to sync transactions` | Press `Retry` on the message, or the re-sync button on the bank's row. If it keeps failing, see below |

### A bank keeps failing to sync

Bank consents expire. When yours runs out, or your bank wants you to confirm access again, every sync of that bank fails with `Failed to sync transactions`, and Freenary has no reconnect button. Instead:

1. Press the re-sync button on the bank's row once more. A temporary failure often clears.
2. If it still fails, [remove the bank](#remove-a-bank). Its accounts and transactions go with it.
3. Press `Connect` on the same bank and approve access again.
