A dashboard is a page of widgets you come back to, such as your spending by week beside your upcoming bills. The assistant builds it from a chat in [Ask](/docs/use/ask), and its widgets read your data each time you open it, so the figures stay current.

## Build a dashboard

Ask for one in any of these ways:

- In a chat, ask for one, such as "Build me a dashboard to check in on my month".
- Under an answer that holds a chart, press `Make it a dashboard`.
- In the sidebar, under **Dashboards**, press `New dashboard`. **Ask** opens with a request already started for you to finish. The last card under **Dashboards** on [Home](/docs/use/home) does the same.

The assistant picks the widgets, names the dashboard and saves it. A card under its answer opens the new dashboard. Every dashboard is listed in the sidebar under **Dashboards**, in the `Dashboards` tab of **Ask**, and in the [command menu](/docs/use/ask#search-or-ask).

You keep up to 30 dashboards, each with up to 12 widgets. The assistant can't save one more until you delete one.

## What a dashboard can show

| Widget | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| A headline figure | Spent, income or saved this month, available to spend, what your current accounts hold, or your net worth |
| Spending by week | 4 to 12 weeks of spending, stacked by your top categories |
| Income vs spending | Money in and money out, month by month, over 3 to 12 months |
| Budgets | This month's spending against each line of your plan |
| Top categories | The 3 to 8 categories you spent the most in this month |
| Upcoming bills | Bills due in the next 7 to 31 days |
| Net worth | Your net worth today and its trend over 3 to 12 months |
| Accounts | Every account with its balance |
| Transactions | Your latest transactions over 1 to 12 months, for some categories or one company if you ask |

A widget that Home also shows counts its figures the same way: see [This month](/docs/use/home#this-month). The assistant can give a widget a heading of its own.

## Open a dashboard

A dashboard's page shows its name and, under it, `From` and the chat it was built in. Press the chat's name to open it.

`Updated` gives the latest time a bank reported a balance. The round button next to it fetches new data from all your banks.

## Change a dashboard

Type what you want in the field at the bottom of the page, `Ask to change this dashboard…`, such as "Add my upcoming bills", and press `Change`. The assistant works in the dashboard's chat, using the model you picked in **Ask**, and its reply shows above the field. Press `Open the chat` to read it in full.

Whenever the assistant saves a dashboard, from this field or from a chat, a pink and gold band sweeps down the dashboard as its new widgets appear. It also passes over the **Dashboards** list in the sidebar and over the dashboard's card in the chat. The band doesn't play when your system asks for reduced motion.

The field stays off until a model is available: see [Choose the model](/docs/use/ask#choose-the-model).

## Rename or delete a dashboard

Press `…` next to the dashboard's name:

- `Rename` takes a new name of up to 60 characters.
- `Delete…` asks you to confirm, then removes the dashboard for good and takes you back to Home. The chat it came from stays in **Ask**.
